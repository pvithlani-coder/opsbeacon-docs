## Weekly score tracking

Beacon calculates your FinOps Health Score each time you run a report or ask for it. Over time, it tracks the history and surfaces trends.

```
@Beacon how has my FinOps score changed this month?
@Beacon show me my score trend for the last 90 days
@Beacon what drove the score change this week?
```

## Score change alerts

You can configure Beacon to alert you when your score drops significantly:

```
@Beacon alert me if my FinOps score drops more than 5 points
```

## What causes score drops

- New security findings that increase your exposure
- Committed spend expiring (RIs or Savings Plans) that reduces coverage
- New AI workloads launching without efficiency tuning
- Increases in idle or underutilized resources
