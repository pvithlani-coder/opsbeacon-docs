## What it will do

The Score Improvement Simulator lets you model the FinOps Health Score impact of specific actions before you take them:

- "What would my score be if I resolved finding-01 and finding-03?"
- "How much would my score improve if I increased RI coverage from 40% to 70%?"
- "What's the score impact of enabling prompt caching for the summarization project?"
