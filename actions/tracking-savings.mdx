## Verified vs estimated savings

OpsBeacon distinguishes between:
- **Estimated savings** — what Beacon predicted before the fix
- **Verified savings** — what actually showed up in the AWS bill after the fix

Verified savings require a finding to be marked resolved and confirmed absent in a subsequent scan. The actual cost reduction is then measured against the baseline.

@Beacon show me verified savings from security findings
