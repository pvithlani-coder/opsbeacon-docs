---
title: Reviewing and Assigning Findings
description: How to review a finding in detail and assign it to a team member.
---

## Reviewing a finding

```
@Beacon review finding-01
@Beacon tell me more about finding-02
```

Beacon returns a detailed breakdown:
- Plain-language explanation of the issue
- The specific AWS resource or configuration involved
- The financial exposure (cost per month if unresolved)
- The recommended fix with step-by-step guidance
- Estimated effort and fix cost

## Assigning a finding

```
@Beacon assign finding-01 to @alice
@Beacon assign finding-02 to @bob with a note: "check with security team first"
```

The assignee receives a Slack notification with the finding details and their action item.

## Bulk assignment

```
@Beacon assign all low-effort security findings to @alice
@Beacon assign findings 01, 02, and 03 to @bob
```
