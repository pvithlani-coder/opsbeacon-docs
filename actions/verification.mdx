## How verification works

When you mark a finding as resolved, Beacon schedules a verification check. On the next scan:

1. Beacon re-runs the relevant check against your AWS account
2. If the finding no longer appears, it's marked **Verified**
3. The financial exposure is removed from your active total
4. The actual cost reduction (if measurable) is recorded as verified savings

## Manual verification trigger

```
@Beacon verify finding-01 now
```

Forces an immediate re-check rather than waiting for the next scheduled scan.

## What if verification fails?

If the finding reappears after being marked resolved:

```
Finding-01 re-opened: GuardDuty still disabled in us-east-1
```

Beacon re-opens the finding, notifies the owner, and restores the exposure to your total.
