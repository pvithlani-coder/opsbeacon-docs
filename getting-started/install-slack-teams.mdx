title: Install OpsBeacon in Slack
description: Add Beacon to your Slack workspace in under five minutes.
---

## Prerequisites

- Slack workspace admin access (required to install apps)
- An OpsBeacon account — [sign up at opsbeacon.co](https://opsbeacon.co)

## Install steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the install page">
    Visit [opsbeacon.co](https://opsbeacon.co) and click **Add to Slack**. You'll be redirected to Slack's OAuth flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the app">
    Review the permissions OpsBeacon requests and click **Allow**. You'll be redirected back to the OpsBeacon setup page.

    OpsBeacon requests the following Slack permissions:
    - `chat:write` — post messages to channels
    - `app_mentions:read` — respond when tagged with `@Beacon`
    - `channels:read` — list channels so you can choose where reports post
    - `users:read` — identify who is asking questions
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your reporting channel">
    Select the Slack channel where you want Beacon to post reports. Most teams use a dedicated `#cloud-costs` or `#finops` channel. You can change this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Beacon to the channel">
    In your chosen Slack channel, type:
    ```
    /invite @Beacon
    ```
    This allows Beacon to post there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AWS account">
    You'll be prompted to connect your AWS account. See [Connect Your AWS Account](/getting-started/connect-aws) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify the installation

Once installed, run a quick test in Slack:

```
@Beacon hello
```

Beacon should respond with a confirmation message and your workspace status.

## Adding Beacon to additional channels

You can invite Beacon to as many channels as you like:

```
/invite @Beacon
```

Beacon will respond to `@Beacon` mentions in any channel it's been invited to.

## Changing the reporting channel

To update where scheduled reports are posted, mention Beacon in the new channel:

```
@Beacon post reports here
```

## Removing OpsBeacon

To remove OpsBeacon from your Slack workspace, go to **Slack Settings → Manage Apps → OpsBeacon → Remove App**. This revokes all Slack permissions. Your AWS connection remains active until you also remove the IAM role from your AWS account.
