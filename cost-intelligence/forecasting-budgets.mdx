## Cost forecasting

Beacon uses your current month's spend trajectory to project your end-of-month total. It factors in historical patterns and any anomalies already detected.

```
@Beacon what will my AWS bill be this month?
@Beacon am I on track to hit my budget?
@Beacon forecast my spend for next quarter
```

## Budget tracking

If you have budgets configured in AWS Budgets, Beacon can track progress against them and alert you when you're approaching a threshold.

```
@Beacon what's my budget utilization this month?
@Beacon am I over budget on any services?
```

## Setting up budget alerts

```
@Beacon alert me when monthly spend exceeds $10,000
@Beacon notify me when EC2 spend goes over $3,000 this month
```
