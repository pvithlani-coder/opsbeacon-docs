## What it shows

When your AWS spend increases, Beacon traces the change down to the specific service, usage type, region, and resource level — so you know what to investigate.

## How to use it

```
@Beacon what's driving my cost increase this month?
@Beacon why did EC2 spend go up 40% last week?
@Beacon what's the most expensive thing in my account right now?
```

## Output format

Beacon returns:
- The primary cost driver (service + usage type)
- The change in absolute dollars and percentage
- The region and account where the change occurred
- Where possible, the specific resource type or operation responsible

## Limitations

Root cause analysis works at the service and usage-type level. Beacon cannot currently identify specific EC2 instance IDs or Lambda function names as cost drivers — that level of detail requires AWS Cost Allocation Tags or AWS Cost Anomaly Detection with resource-level tagging.
