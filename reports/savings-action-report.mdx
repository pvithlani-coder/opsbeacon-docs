## What it shows

The Savings & Action Report gives a status view of all open and resolved recommendations — what was found, who owns it, whether it's been fixed, and whether the fix actually reduced spend.

```
@Beacon show me the savings action report
@Beacon what recommendations are still open?
@Beacon how much have we saved from closed recommendations this quarter?
```
