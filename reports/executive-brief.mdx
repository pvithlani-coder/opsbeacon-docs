## What it shows

The Executive Brief is a one-page summary designed for VPs, CFOs, and engineering leaders who want the bottom line without the technical detail:

- Total AWS spend and trend
- Top 3 savings opportunities with dollar values
- FinOps Health Score and change
- Key actions and who owns them

```
@Beacon give me the executive brief
@Beacon generate a leadership summary of our cloud costs
```

## Formatting for sharing

The Executive Brief is formatted as a clean Slack message that can be forwarded or shared directly in a leadership channel.
