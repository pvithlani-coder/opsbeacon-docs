## Privacy commitments

- OpsBeacon does not sell customer data
- OpsBeacon does not use customer AWS cost or security data to train models
- OpsBeacon does not share customer data with third parties except as required to operate the service (AWS infrastructure, Slack)

## Your controls

- **Disconnect AWS** — delete the IAM role at any time to immediately revoke all AWS access
- **Remove from Slack** — remove the Slack app to immediately revoke all Slack access
- **Request data deletion** — email support@opsbeacon.co to request deletion of all stored data
- **Export your data** — contact support to request an export of your historical score and finding data

## Compliance

OpsBeacon is designed for compliance with SOC 2 Type II controls. Formal certification is in progress. Contact support@opsbeacon.co for the current compliance documentation.
